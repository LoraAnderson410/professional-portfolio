Employee Experience & Retention

Redesigning the First 90 Days

Redesigned onboarding for employees in residential youth services through structured training, peer mentorship, and leadership support. The program created a more gradual transition into demanding direct care work and additional opportunities for employees to raise concerns.

90% → 30% Approximate first 90 day turnover at one home before and after implementation. Historical estimates, not a controlled measure of causation.

Other Solutions I've Developed

Not every project becomes a formal case study. These examples reflect other systems, processes, and improvements I've worked on throughout my career.

Human Resources & Technology

HRIS Implementation & Organizational Change

Supported transitions from existing HR processes to systems including UKG, ADP Workforce Now, and Paychex. Worked across HR, payroll, employee records, and operational processes to align system capabilities with organizational needs. These implementations required coordinating technology changes with the people responsible for using and maintaining the systems.

Vendor Management & Administration

Contract Lifecycle Management

Developed a centralized process for monitoring organizational vendor contracts and important renewal dates. Created a tracking system with reminders to improve visibility into upcoming decisions and reduce reliance on individual memory. The approach supported more deliberate vendor management and contract oversight.

Analytics & Decision Support

Operational Performance Dashboards

Developed reporting tools and dashboards to organize operational and workforce information into more useful measures. Worked with Excel and Power BI to make performance indicators easier to review and discuss. The focus was on providing leadership with information that could support better decisions.

Property Operations & Technology

Property Management System Implementation

Participated in selecting and implementing Buildium to support property management operations. The transition involved moving away from paper based practices toward more centralized digital processes. This required considering the needs of staff, property operations, and the information used to manage daily work.

Safety & Compliance

Safety Management & Compliance Processes

Worked across workplace safety, regulatory compliance, training, and operational risk. Developed and supported processes intended to make safety responsibilities more consistent and visible within organizations. My approach has emphasized that effective safety programs require practical execution and accountability, not simply written policies.

Workforce Planning & Operations

Workforce Scheduling & Staffing Processes

Worked with staffing and scheduling processes in environments where coverage, training, and employee availability affected operations. Applied structured planning to help balance staffing needs with required training and day to day service demands. These efforts required coordination among managers and employees rather than treating scheduling as an isolated administrative task.
