Professional Experience

Professional Solutions

Every organization has its own challenges, resources, and limitations. I enjoy figuring out what's actually causing a problem, understanding what an organization needs, and developing a solution that works within those realities.

Sometimes the answer is a new system. Other times, it's finding a creative way to use the tools already available. I've always found the most satisfaction in turning a problem into something practical that people can actually use. These are some of the solutions I've developed throughout my career.

Employee Experience & Retention

Redesigning the First 90 Days

One residential youth services home was losing approximately 90% of new employees within their first three months. The existing onboarding approach wasn't preparing or supporting staff adequately for demanding work.

I redesigned the process using existing training resources, experienced volunteer mentors, phased responsibilities, and structured leadership conversations. The approach focused on employee readiness and support rather than introducing another technology platform.

90% → 30% Approximate first 90 day turnover at one home before and after implementation. Historical estimates, not proof of a single cause.

Workflow Automation & Compliance

Policy Lifecycle Management & Automation

Hundreds of organizational policies required recurring reviews, document collection, compliance assessment, approval tracking, and publication. The manual process depended heavily on administrative follow through.

I connected existing Microsoft 365 tools to automate review requests, document routing, status updates, and notifications. Compliance review and board approval remained human responsibilities, while the routine administrative work became more systematic.

~300 Policies Supported by connected workflows operating for approximately two years. Time savings were not formally measured.

Additional Implementations

Practical Solutions Across the Organization

Not every solution needs a formal case study. These examples show how I've addressed different operational challenges, often by making better use of existing technology, information, and resources.

Financial Controls & Accountability

Financial Controls & Automation

Agency Credit Card Management

Agency credit cards were being issued without a formal process, creating unnecessary accountability risks. I developed a credit card policy, a documented card issuance process, and receipt collection procedures. I also built a macro enabled Excel tracker that incorporated monthly credit card statements and provided spending dashboards by location and cardholder.

Project Management & Financial Analysis

Construction Budget & Project Tracking

Renovation projects needed clearer oversight of budgets and progress, but purchasing project management software wasn't an option. I built an Excel tracking system, wrote a query to incorporate updated QuickBooks data each month, and created a dashboard showing budget performance and the status of individual construction activities.

Development Operations

Fundraising & Donor Tracking

Fundraising information needed to be organized and accessible, but the budget didn't support a dedicated CRM. I developed a tracking solution using Excel and Access to manage information with tools already available to the organization. It provided a practical alternative to purchasing another system.

Vendor Management & Risk Reduction

Contract Lifecycle Management

Vendor contracts and renewal dates needed a more reliable tracking process than depending on individual reminders or scattered records. I developed a centralized contract tracker with automated reminders using available tools. The system helped bring upcoming contract decisions into view before deadlines arrived.

Technology & Operational Systems

Human Resources & Systems

HRIS Selection & Implementation

Different organizations needed HR systems suited to their size, operations, and administrative requirements. I've been involved in implementing Paychex and UKG Ready. At NBAC, I led the evaluation of approximately a dozen HRIS options before selecting and implementing ADP Workforce Now for the organization.

IT Governance & Information Security

SharePoint Access Audit & Cleanup

SharePoint files and access permissions needed a thorough audit, but the available tools were limited. I developed a PowerShell script to identify files and access information, then led the manual cleanup of the findings. The solution combined technical investigation with the detailed work needed to address problems.

Facilities & Operations

Maintenance Request Tracking

Maintenance activities needed a consistent way to capture requests and monitor outstanding work. A dedicated maintenance portal wasn't an available option. I developed an alternative tracking process using existing resources to provide better visibility without the expense of another platform.

Property Operations & Technology

Property Management System Implementation

Paper based practices made property management information and daily administrative activities harder to coordinate. I helped select and implement Buildium to centralize information and support the organization's property management processes. The transition required considering existing workflows and how staff would use the system.

Safety, Compliance & Employee Support

Workplace Safety & Reporting

Safety Monitoring & Incident Reporting

Safety oversight involved inspections, drills, and workplace incidents that needed consistent tracking and management visibility. I developed an Excel based system with automated monthly email reports. Microsoft Forms incident submissions were also automated into the safety spreadsheet, reducing manual data handling.

Training Compliance & Analytics

Training Monitoring & Automated Reports

Managers needed timely information about training activity and compliance requirements. I developed reporting processes that organized training data and automatically distributed monthly reports by email. The reports provided regular information for monitoring completion and identifying areas needing attention.

AI Governance & Confidentiality

Organizational AI Use Policy

I discovered employees entering sensitive clinical and personnel information into public AI tools, raising serious privacy and confidentiality concerns. I developed an organizational AI policy to address appropriate use and information protection. The goal was to establish practical safeguards while recognizing that AI was becoming part of everyday work.

Emergency Preparedness & Facilities

Emergency Evacuation Map Redesign

Existing evacuation maps were hand drawn, visually inconsistent, and difficult for people to follow. Specialized mapping software couldn't be justified financially. I used Excel to create clearer, more professional evacuation maps using a tool the organization already owned.

Knowledge Management & Training

Training Manuals & Standard Procedures

Important job knowledge can be lost or inconsistently communicated when employees change positions or leave an organization. I've developed detailed operating procedures and training resources, including a complete accounts payable manual, program staff instructions, and IT guides. The goal was to make knowledge easier to transfer and daily processes easier to follow.

Workforce Planning & Operations

Staffing & Training Coordination

Staffing in residential services required balancing coverage, employee readiness, and mandatory training requirements. I helped coordinate staffing approaches that allowed employees to complete training while maintaining service coverage. The work required cooperation among managers and experienced staff across locations.

Finding What Works

I don't believe every challenge requires new software, another consultant, or a larger budget. Sometimes those investments make sense. Other times, the best solution is a spreadsheet, an automation, a clearer process, or a better way to use existing information.

The important part is understanding the problem, recognizing the organization's requirements and limitations, and creating something people can actually use and maintain. That's the kind of work I enjoy.

These examples are drawn from actual professional experience. Confidential information and proprietary organizational materials have been excluded. The featured case studies provide additional implementation details and explain the limitations of reported results.
