Employee Experience & Retention

Redesigning the First 90 Days

A residential youth services program was struggling with significant employee turnover. At one home, approximately 90% of new direct care staff were leaving within their first 90 days.

I redesigned the onboarding experience to provide stronger preparation, mentorship, and leadership support during those critical first three months.

The new approach included:

  • 60 hours of training completed before the first floor shift
  • A structured 30, 60, and 90 day progression toward independent work
  • Experienced volunteer mentors and scheduled leadership conversations
  • Improved staffing coordination and training accountability
90% → 30%

Approximate first 90 day turnover at the highest turnover home before and after implementation.

Historical site estimates. The observed improvement is not attributed solely to the onboarding redesign.

Process Automation & Compliance

Policy Lifecycle Management & Workflow Automation

Managing approximately 300 organizational policies required extensive manual tracking, document routing, follow up, and coordination of review deadlines.

I designed and implemented an automated workflow using Excel, Microsoft Power Automate, Outlook, OneDrive, and SharePoint.

The solution included:

  • Automated policy review requests based on calculated dates
  • Document routing and tracking updates triggered by email and file activity
  • Notifications for approved policies and publication handoffs
  • Failure alerts and human checkpoints for compliance review and board approval
~300 Policies

Supported by connected workflows that remained in operational use for approximately two years.

Time savings and financial benefits were not formally measured.
