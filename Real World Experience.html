Leadership in Action

Professional Solutions

Operations leadership means connecting people, systems, and financial decisions to the organization's goals. Throughout my career, I've entered unfamiliar environments, learned how their operations really work, and found practical ways to improve them. Sometimes that calls for new technology; often it means making better use of the people, tools, and budgets already available. The examples below show both the results and the resourcefulness behind them.

Onboarding & Employee Retention

Finance · Featured Case Study

Property Portfolio Growth & Strategic Redevelopment

National Builders and Acceptance Corporation

100% Growth in Rental Revenue

Led a strategy to improve occupancy and rental performance across a portfolio of more than 100 units. Prioritized vacant properties, directed targeted renovations, and developed marketing strategies tailored to students, professionals, and medical workers. Oversaw three full-building renovations and numerous apartment improvements, including converting a vacant three-unit building into four rental units.
