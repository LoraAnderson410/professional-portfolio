About Me

Curious by nature. Solution focused by habit.

I've spent most of my career figuring out how to make organizations work better. I've led teams, managed operations, and worked across HR, IT, compliance, facilities, finance, data, and administration. I usually end up somewhere in the middle, connecting the pieces.

I like understanding how a business actually works, not just how the process says it works. I ask a lot of questions, look for what is getting in the way, and start thinking about how we could make it simpler, smarter, or more sustainable.

I'm also endlessly curious. If I don't know how to do something, my first instinct is usually to figure it out. A lot of what I've learned has started with a real problem, a new tool, or a question I wanted answered.

What I'm Building

Northstar IT

I built Northstar because I wanted one realistic place to keep learning and connect the different parts of a business instead of practicing each skill in isolation.

It started as a way to work with data, but it quickly grew into much more, including operations, workforce planning, governance, process design, SQL, reporting, policies, and all the little decisions that make a company actually run.

I keep expanding it because every time I answer one question, I usually find three more.
