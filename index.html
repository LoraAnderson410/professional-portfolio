About Me

My Approach to Leadership

I believe effective leadership requires understanding more than what's happening within one department. Decisions about people, finances, technology, and operations rarely exist in isolation, and sustainable improvement depends on understanding how those pieces fit together.

I'm a hands-on leader who values accountability, honest communication, and creating environments where people can do their best work. I'm willing to ask difficult questions, challenge long-standing practices, and address problems rather than work around them.

I'm also naturally curious. I enjoy learning and exploring new technology, and that curiosity continues to inform how I lead.
