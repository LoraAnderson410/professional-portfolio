Professional Recommendations

What Others Say About My Work

Perspective from people who have worked with me throughout my career.

A resume can describe responsibilities and accomplishments. Recommendations add something different: the perspective of people who have actually worked alongside me and can speak to how I lead, solve problems, communicate, and contribute.

These letters come from different points in my career and different types of organizations, but together they provide a broader view of how I show up at work.

Beyond Job Titles

Experience is also about how people experience working with you.

My career has included operations, administration, risk, people leadership, systems, analytics, process improvement, and cross-functional work.

The responsibilities have changed from one organization to another, but the way I approach the work has been fairly consistent: understand the problem, take ownership, communicate clearly, build practical solutions, and try to leave the organization stronger than I found it.

These recommendations provide independent perspective on those qualities from people who have worked with me in different roles and at different stages of my career.
