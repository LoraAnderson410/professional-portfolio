Featured Case Study

Northstar IT

A fictional company I built so I could keep learning by actually doing the work.

Northstar started because I wanted something more realistic than practicing SQL queries or building random dashboards with disconnected data. I learn best when there is a real problem to solve, so I built a fictional B2B technology company with employees, customers, processes, policies, systems, financial activity, and plenty of operational messiness.

The project keeps growing because every time I answer one question, I usually find three more. That is part of what I like about it.

Why I Built It

I wanted one place where all the pieces could connect.

My background has never fit neatly into one box. I have worked across operations, HR, IT, compliance, facilities, reporting, process improvement, risk, and leadership. Most of the time I am happiest when I am somewhere in the middle, figuring out how all of those pieces affect each other.

Northstar gives me a place to do exactly that. Instead of saying I know SQL, process mapping, analytics, governance, or operations, I can actually build something and show how I used them together.

It is also a learning project. I am using it to deepen my work with MariaDB, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, database design, data validation, and business analysis.

The part I care about most is not the tool itself. It is what the information helps me understand and what decision I would make differently because of it.
