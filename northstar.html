Northstar IT

From Business Design to Operational Decisions

Northstar IT is a fictional B2B technology company I created as a working environment for business planning, operations, systems design, data architecture, analytics, and continuous learning.

Business Design Operations Governance Data Architecture Analytics & BI
Why Northstar Exists

A place to keep thinking, learning, and building.

I created Northstar IT because I wanted a realistic environment where I could keep building my technical skills. I also wanted something that would keep me thinking, learning, and solving real problems.

My professional background spans operations, compliance, administration, process improvement, people leadership, systems, reporting, and business analysis. Northstar gives me a place to connect those areas instead of presenting them as unrelated skills on a resume.

The project also gives me room to learn tools and concepts I have not worked with as deeply before. That includes MariaDB, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, relational database design, and integrated business analytics.

The goal is not just to build dashboards. It is to understand how strategy, people, processes, systems, data, and reporting work together. Then I can use that information to make better operational decisions.
