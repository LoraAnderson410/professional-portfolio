Northstar IT

From Business Design to Operational Decisions

Northstar IT is a fictional B2B technology company I created as a working environment for business planning, operations, governance, process design, relational data, SQL analysis, business intelligence, and continuous learning.

Rather than developing isolated technical exercises, I am building Northstar as an interconnected business environment where strategy, people, processes, systems, data, and reporting influence one another.

Why Northstar Exists

A place to keep thinking, learning, and building.

I created Northstar IT because I wanted a realistic environment where I could continue developing both technical and analytical skills while solving the kinds of business problems that interest me.

My professional background spans operations, compliance, administration, process improvement, people leadership, systems, reporting, analytics, and business analysis. Northstar gives me a place to connect those disciplines rather than presenting them as unrelated skills on a resume.

The project also gives me room to deepen my experience with MariaDB, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, relational database design, data validation, analytics, and integrated business reporting.

The goal is not simply to produce dashboards or technical files. The goal is to understand what the data means, identify the operational story behind it, and translate those findings into decisions leadership could actually use.
